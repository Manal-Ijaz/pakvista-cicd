Lahore – City of Gardens

Lahore is the cultural heart of Pakistan, famous for its rich history, vibrant atmosphere, and magnificent Mughal architecture. It is a city that beautifully blends the ancient with the modern, offering an unforgettable experience to travelers.

Badshahi Mosque

The Badshahi Mosque, commissioned by the Mughal Emperor Aurangzeb in 1671, is one of the most iconic landmarks in Lahore. Built with stunning red sandstone and adorned with white marble domes, it stood as the largest mosque in the world for over three centuries.

Lahore Fort

Located immediately opposite the Badshahi Mosque, the Lahore Fort (Shahi Qila) is a masterpiece of Mughal history. This sprawling citadel houses multiple breathtaking structures, including the famous Sheesh Mahal (Palace of Mirrors), which reflects the architectural brilliance of the Mughal era.

Shalimar Gardens

A designated UNESCO World Heritage site, the Shalimar Gardens were laid out in 1641 as a Persian paradise garden. Built by Emperor Shah Jahan, the gardens are celebrated for their extensive waterworks, terraced layouts, beautiful pavilions, and hundreds of fountains.
